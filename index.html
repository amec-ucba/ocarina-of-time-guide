Page title: Ocarina of Time Guide: Ocarina of Time on Nintendo Switch 2 Page description: A fan-made guide to the Ocarina of Time remake on Nintendo Switch 2, with controls, ocarina songs, and a walkthrough of the first dungeon. Ocarina of Time Guide Home Controls Ocarina Songs Deku Tree Walkthrough Welcome to Hyrule In The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, you play as Link, a young boy who grows up in Kokiri Forest. When the Great Deku Tree is cursed, Link sets out on a quest that takes him across Hyrule and seven years into the future to stop the evil Ganondorf. Young Link and Navi in Kokiri Forest, rebuilt for Nintendo Switch 2. About the Remake Ocarina of Time first came out on the Nintendo 64 in 1998 and was remade for the Nintendo 3DS in 2011. This new version, released on November 5, 2026, was rebuilt from the ground up for Nintendo Switch 2. It adds a jump button, a free camera, full voice acting, and an orchestral soundtrack. You can even hum the ocarina songs into the Switch 2 microphone. What's in This Guide Controls & Saving What every button does, how to save, and the settings worth changing first. Ocarina Songs The button inputs for four essential songs, written out on a music staff. Deku Tree Walkthrough A step-by-step route through the first dungeon, including how to beat Queen Gohma.
