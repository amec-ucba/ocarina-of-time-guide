Page title: Controls: Ocarina of Time Guide Page description: Every default button in the Ocarina of Time remake for Nintendo Switch 2, and how to save your game. Ocarina of Time Guide Home Controls Ocarina Songs Deku Tree Walkthrough Controls The remake plays like a modern adventure game. Link can finally jump whenever he wants, and the camera turns freely. The Buttons Left Stick moves Link. Right Stick turns the camera. Press it in to reset the view. X jumps. Y swings your sword. A talks, opens, and picks things up. Hold it to dash. B puts away the item you are holding. ZL targets an enemy. Hold it to raise your shield. ZR uses your selected item, like the Slingshot. D-Pad Left takes out the ocarina. + opens the menu, and − opens the map. Saving Your Game The game saves on its own at many points, but it is smart to save before a boss fight and before you quit: Press + to open the menu. Press L or R to switch to the System tab. Choose Save. Rather Hum Than Press Buttons? You can play every ocarina song by humming into the Switch 2 microphone. Learn the songs on the Ocarina Songs page.
