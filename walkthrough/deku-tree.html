Ocarina of Time Guide Home Controls Ocarina Songs Deku Tree Walkthrough Inside the Deku Tree The Great Deku Tree is the first dungeon in the game. It teaches the basics you will use for the rest of the adventure: aiming at faraway targets, burning things in your way, and finding paths between floors. Jump to: Before You Go, The Route, or The Boss. Before You Go Mido will not let you near the Great Deku Tree until you have a sword & shield. Find the Kokiri Sword and buy the Deku Shield in Kokiri Forest first. The Route Find the Fairy Slingshot. Climb up through the tree. When a Deku Scrub spits a seed at you, block it with your shield to bounce it back. In the next room, climb the vine walls to the chest that holds the Fairy Slingshot. Drop down to the basement. Jump onto the giant web in the middle of the tree. It breaks, and you fall into the basement. Burn your way through the basement. Step on the floor switch to light a torch, then light a Deku Stick on it. Use the burning stick to burn the web blocking the next room. Cross the water room. Dive down and hit the switch to lower the water. Ride the platform across, then light the torches and crawl through the vent. Burn the web in the floor to drop down one more level. Defeat the three Deku Scrubs in the right order to open the boss door. Pro Tip A Deku Scrub in the basement tells you a secret code: 2, 3, 1. Remember it. Near the boss, you need to bounce seeds back at three Deku Scrubs in that order: the middle one, then the right, then the left. The Boss: Queen Gohma Queen Gohma drops from the ceiling as soon as you look up. As soon as you walk in, look up. Queen Gohma is waiting on the ceiling. When her eye turns red, hit it with the Slingshot to stun her, then run in and attack her eye with your sword. If she climbs back up to lay eggs, break them before they hatch. Beat her to earn a Heart Container. The Great Deku Tree then gives you the Kokiri's Emerald, the first of three Spiritual Stones.
