Page title: Ocarina Songs: Ocarina of Time Guide Page description: Switch 2 button inputs for Zelda's Lullaby, Saria's Song, Epona's Song, and the Song of Storms in the Ocarina of Time remake. Ocarina of Time Guide Home Controls Ocarina Songs Deku Tree Walkthrough Ocarina Songs The ocarina has five notes, and each one is a button. From lowest to highest, they are L, R, A, Y, and X. Each staff below has one row per button, with the highest note on top. Read it from left to right, or use the button list under each staff. Zelda's Lullaby Opens doors and passages marked with the royal family's crest. It also works on the cows at Lon Lon Ranch. X |---X-----------------X------------------| Y |---------Y-----------------Y------------| A |---------------A-----------------A------| R |----------------------------------------| L |----------------------------------------| Play: X Y A X Y A Song of Storms Makes it rain. It also gets the windmill in Kakariko Village spinning. X |---------------X-----------------X------| Y |----------------------------------------| A |----------------------------------------| R |---------R-----------------R------------| L |---L-----------------L------------------| Play: L R X L R X Hum It Instead New in the remake, you can skip the buttons and hum a song into the Switch 2 microphone. The game listens for the melody and plays the matching song. It works best in a quiet room. Forgot a song? Hold ZL while playing the ocarina to see every song you have learned.
